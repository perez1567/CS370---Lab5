Product Backlog Requirements
—---------------------------

Must-Have features: 
- Messaging system allowing app users and care providers to communicate: 
    ->Plan future appointments 
    ->Update users on prescription needs & refill dates
- Users can take photos and send to care providers the following:  
    ->Updates on injury recovery 
    ->Newly found infections  
    ->Obstacles preventing positive recovery
 
Nice-to-Have features: 
- Each individual will receive a pop-up notification on screen when… 
    ->Message is received by other user 
    ->Future appointment(s) updated / approaching soon
- Application has a background with useful information, color, and animation
    ->Date, username, tabs displayed 
    ->Can maximize or minimize window without restricting access to software
